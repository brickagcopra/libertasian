- (a) Restore all four as design-aware variants of `DigestDetailScreen` (significant work — needs design extension, probably 4 new sub-components), OR
- (b) Add a `digest.digestType !== 'case_digest'` fallback that renders a bare-bones legacy view for now, with a TODO to design it properly later.

DD5-DD10 are compliance/trust surfaces and the same CLAUDE.md provenance rule from RD4 applies — **strong recommendation to restore at least DD5 (ContentDisclaimer) and DD7-DD10 (the four trust badges) unconditionally.**

DD6 (ExportButton) is a paid-tier feature — losing it silently could affect billing/entitlements UX.
